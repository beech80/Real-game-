# REAL — Player guide

## Controls (phone)

- **Left thumb:** joystick to walk. Tap **RUN** to sprint.
- **Right thumb:** drag to turn the camera. Pinch to zoom.
- **Gold button:** whatever is near you, such as doors, people, signs, counters and work stations.
- **📱 button:** your phone and its apps.

## The journey

1. **Prologue:** The Club revokes your membership.
2. **Chapter 1:** you walk from the empty road to the city, reading signs on the way.
3. **Chapter 2:** the Community Center finds you housing and gives you $100 and a phone.
4. **Chapter 3:** you apply at Fresh Mart and work your first shift.
5. **Chapter 4:** you meet Jax.
6. **Chapter 5:** daily life, with rent, bills, cooking and sleep. 1 real minute ≈ 10 game minutes.
7. **Chapters 6–9:** grow your **Life Level** to open new districts by bus:
   - Midtown at Life Level 3
   - Opportunity District at Life Level 5
   - The Heights at Life Level 8

   Then walk into any building to apply for jobs, take classes, adopt a pet, buy a vehicle, lease or buy a home, start a business, or clear challenge zones.
8. **Chapters 10–12:** Jax's challenge comes first. At Life Level 6 The Club invites you back, and you choose **Return, Reject or Rebuild**.
9. **Epilogue:** the city stays open, so keep living.

## Learning

Every task in the game is a real skill. Hints are always free, there is no timer, and there are no grades. Skills move from *Learning* to *Practicing*, then *Proficient*, then *Mastered* as you use them independently in different places on different days.
