## How every skill is taught

Discover (first encounter in the world) → Understand (explanation card) → Practice (guided, hints) → Apply (independent use in a real task) → Master (correct without hints, in 2+ different places, on a later day). Mastered skills come back for spaced review after 3, 7, 14 and 30 days. Mistakes show a worked explanation, switch to the alternate explanation, and after repeated misses point to an unmastered prerequisite. Speed is never measured.

Activity types: choice, number entry, ordering steps, sorting, open writing with rubric feedback, budget allocation, cursive tracing (with a type-instead alternative).
