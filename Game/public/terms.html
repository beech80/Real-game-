Terms of use

Last updated: November 2026

For kids: Play fair, be kind, and keep private things private. If something in the game feels wrong or unsafe, tell a grown-up you trust.

1. What REAL is

REAL is a free learning game. It teaches school subjects and life skills through a pretend city. Money, jobs, banks, loans and businesses in the game are pretend and teach ideas; nothing in the game is real money or real advice.

2. Free, no purchases

There are no in-game purchases, ads or subscriptions. Rewards are earned by playing and learning.

3. Learning help and answers

Lessons, explanations and Heart’s answers aim to be accurate and age-appropriate, but they can be wrong sometimes. They are not medical, legal, financial or safety advice. For real-life decisions, ask a trusted adult or a professional. Where the game runs on claude.ai, some answers are written by Claude (an AI by Anthropic), which can make mistakes.

4. Playing kindly and safely

  • Don’t type real names, addresses, schools, phone numbers or passwords into the game.
  • Live neighbors can only see anonymous characters and preset gestures; there is no chat.
  • Don’t try to break, overload or copy the game to harm others.

5. Your saves

Saves live on your device. Clearing browser data or deleting the app removes them, so keep a save file or save code if you want a backup.

6. Schools and families

Teachers and parents choose whether and how children use REAL, and should read the privacy page. School mode turns off live neighbors and smart character replies.

7. The game, as it is

REAL is provided as it is, without guarantees that it will always work or be error-free. The voice recordings were made with open-source voices (Kokoro and Piper); fonts are from Google Fonts under their open licences.
