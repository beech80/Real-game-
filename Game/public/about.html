📚Every subject, every levelReading, writing, cursive and print, math, money, science, thinking, social studies, health and technology, from kindergarten to high school. It adapts to each learner.
✨Heart, the reading voiceA natural voice reads every lesson and story, cheers, explains why an answer isn’t right, and answers questions, in English and Spanish.
🧑‍🏫Never stuckHints, “Show me how” worked examples, another way to explain it, math you can move, and spaced review so learning sticks.
🔊Made for every learnerTap any word to hear it and sound it out, speak your answers, bigger text, high contrast, keyboard-only play, and a calm, no-timer design.

👪 For families
  • No account, no email, no ads, no in-game purchases.
  • Progress is saved on your device. Move it with a save file or save code.
  • The Family view shows what your child learned this week, where they’re stuck and how to help.
  • Install it like an app, or download one file to play offline.
🍎 For teachers
  • School mode, standards-aligned skills, and a class dashboard.
  • Assign units with a pre-check and post-check, and see growth.
  • Students share a report code. No student accounts or emails needed.
  • Writing workshop with rubric feedback and a portfolio.
